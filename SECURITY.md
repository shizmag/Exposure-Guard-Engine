# Security Policy

## Responsible Use

ExposureGuard Engine is a **defensive outside-in inspection tool**. It is designed for website owners, engineering teams, and platform operators to audit their own internet-facing exposure and detect state changes across time.

ExposureGuard does not perform offensive exploitation, password guessing, automated form submissions, or denial-of-service tests.

Users are expected to scan targets they own or have explicit authorization to inspect.

---

## Reporting Vulnerabilities in ExposureGuard

If you discover a security vulnerability in ExposureGuard Engine itself (including potential SSRF bypasses, parser panics, or unintended network access):

1. **Do not open a public issue.**
2. Send an email describing the vulnerability, proof-of-concept, and impact to:
   `security@exposureguard.com`
3. We acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you on a coordinated disclosure timeline.
